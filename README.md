# Grady Thompson

PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at
the University of British Columbia.

My research focuses on stochastic processes, optimization,
online algorithms, and machine learning for decision-making
under uncertainty.

## Research Interests

- Applied probability and stochastic processes
- Mathematical optimization and online algorithms
- Scheduling and computing systems
- Reinforcement learning
- Quantitative research

## Selected Projects

- [Quantitative Research](https://github.com/GradyThompson/quant_research)
  — Stochastic price modeling, numerical first-passage
  methods, and statistical forecasting.

- [Serverless Scheduling Research](https://github.com/GradyThompson/serverless-scheduling-research)
  — Online scheduling algorithms, competitive analysis,
  and reinforcement learning.

- [Image Labeler](https://github.com/GradyThompson/image_labeler)
  — Interactive image processing and annotation software
  for confocal microscopy.
